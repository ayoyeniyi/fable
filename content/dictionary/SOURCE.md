## What `webster1913.json` keeps

Only headwords the four books use: 8245 headwords, plus 4238 inflected forms pointing at them. For each, the part of speech and the first 3 senses, each cut to about 240 characters. Etymologies, pronunciations, quotations and compound phrases are dropped.

Coverage: 11425 of the 12898 distinct words in the four books (89%). The rest are mostly names (Dorothy, Telemachus, Lilliput) and coinages; Define says so instead of guessing.

The 1913 definitions are a century old. That suits these books, but some senses read oddly to a modern reader, and a few entries carry the prejudices of their time.

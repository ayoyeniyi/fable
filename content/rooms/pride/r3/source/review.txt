round 1:
The room reads as a library, but the pictures are blank, the walls behind and beside the reader are bare, and everything is too warm for a grey afternoon.

CRITIQUE:
- View 1 and View 3: both gilt frames show flat featureless canvases, so the landscape behind the page and the portrait over the mantel read as empty frames.
- View 4: the south wall between the door and the oak bookcase is a large bare plaster field above the dado, with nothing on it or against it.
- All views: the colour is peach/orange throughout, wrong for "soft grey daylight". The plaster, curtains and upholstery are too warm, the fire spill is strong, and the windows blow out to white (View 2).
- View 5 and View 1: the rug is a saturated salmon-orange on a large plain floor. The rest of the boards are empty, with nothing in front of the hearth.
- View 2: the pier between the two west windows is a plain panel field.
- View 1: the small table under the landscape sits alone and centred, with the four bookcases evenly spaced around it.

round 2:
The room reads as a period library, but it fails on light and finish. The warm cast and bare walls go against the grey afternoon and the book-lined retreat the place describes.

CRITIQUE:
- In views 1 and 3 a warm peach-orange cast lies over the ceiling, walls and floor. That reads as evening firelight, not soft grey afternoon daylight.
- In view 1, the walls show more bare plaster than books. The north wall has two small bookcases with blank plaster around them, and view 2's west wall corner and pier are empty. It does not feel like a room lined with well-used books.
- In view 5, the floor is a wide, pale, featureless expanse, and the rug is a flat pink slab with a grey band.
- In view 1, the bookcases are identical freestanding units evenly spaced along the walls. They look like furniture placed in a room, not shelving built along it.
- In view 2, the pier between the windows has one small dark picture and nothing below it, so the window wall looks unfinished.
- In view 3, nothing sits round the hearth (no fire irons or log basket), so the fireplace reads as staged.

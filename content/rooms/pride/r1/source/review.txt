round 1:
The worst problems are a puddle and the bare path strip at the reader's feet, a sparse and plain meadow, and smooth sausage-like hedges. I moved the reader just off the path, made the grass much denser near the reader and broke up the hedges with shrubs, stems and more hedgerow trees. I also lightened the puddles so they read as reflecting the grey sky, and kept the stile, the gate and Netherfield where they were.

CRITIQUE:
- View 5: a grey-blue puddle lies right under the reader's feet. In view 1 the dark path strip runs straight into the bottom of the page, so the reader appears to stand in the mud.
- Views 1, 3 and 4: the meadow is mostly bare flat green with a thin scatter of blades. It does not read as wet, bent grass after rain.
- Views 1 and 2: the near hedge is a smooth, sausage-like mass with a uniform top and few breaks, not an overgrown, layered hedgerow.
- Views 3 and 4: the cart track reads as disconnected flat brown blobs. The puddles are dull brown-grey and do not reflect the pale sky.
- View 4: behind the reader is an empty field with nothing to break it, and only two stubby bare trees stand in the hedgerows.
- View 1: the distant broadleaf trees around the house are blocky lumps; the house itself sits well.

round 2:
The worst problems are the left hedge, which fills the left view as a blobby wall, and Netherfield sitting dead centre behind the page. Puddles that render as white specks and a bare right-hand field come next.

CRITIQUE:
- View 2 and the left of View 1: the left hedge is only about 4 m away and fills the whole view as one smooth, lumpy green tube with holes in it.
- View 1: Netherfield sits dead centre behind the page, a single object straight behind it, instead of off to one side in the middle distance.
- Views 3 and 4: the puddles show as tiny white flecks like scraps of paper, not dull pools reflecting an overcast sky; the puddle colour is far too bright.
- View 3: the field to the right is almost empty: flat grass, a plain fence and a mud strip with no hedge, trees or tussocks beyond.
- View 1: the distant trees are small identical lollipops spaced evenly along the horizon, which reads as toy-like, not a hedgerow landscape.
- Views 1 and 5: the grass is a thin scatter of blades over a uniform green ground; it needs denser, wetter-looking cover and a more varied ground colour.
